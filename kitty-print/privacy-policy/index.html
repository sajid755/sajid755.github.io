Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

SholoBit ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Kitty Print: Mini Cat Printer mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Data You Create — Stored Locally Only

All user-generated content is stored locally on your device in an SQLite database. We never transmit, sync, or back up this data to any server we own.

This includes:

  • Shop name, address, phone number, and footer text (your receipt settings)
  • Product names, prices, stock levels, and categories
  • Receipt and transaction history, including line items, discounts, tax, and totals
  • Recipient name and phone number optionally added to receipts
  • Customer names optionally stored on loyalty cards (used to personalise printed cards)
  • To-do tasks and their statuses
  • Images selected for printing (processed in memory only — not saved by the app)
  • Pixel art designs you create

If you uninstall the app, all locally stored data is permanently deleted.

1.2 Data Sent When You Submit Feedback

When you use the in-app feedback form, the following info is sent to a Google Forms endpoint operated by Sholobit:

  • The feedback text, type, and contact details you enter
  • Your device model and manufacturer (e.g. "Samsung Galaxy S23")
  • Your Android OS version (e.g. "Android 14")
  • The app version number (e.g. "1.0.0")
  • A timestamp

This data is used solely to diagnose bugs and improve the app. It is not shared with third parties beyond Google (as the form host).

1.3 Data Collected by Third-Party Services

Google Play Billing: Used to process the one-time premium upgrade. We receive confirmation that a purchase occurred but never see your payment details.

Google AdMob: Free users see advertisements served by AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (advertising ID) to serve ads. You can opt out in your Device Settings → Google → Ads.

2. Permissions

Permission Why it is needed
BLUETOOTH, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, SCAN Connect to MXW01 / C9 thermal printers via BLE. No data is sent to our servers.
CAMERA Take new photos directly within the app for printing.
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Required by Android for BLE device scanning. No location data is read or transmitted.
STORAGE Save exported Excel files to your Downloads folder (Android 12 and below).
INTERNET Ad serving, purchase verification, and feedback form.

3. Device Integrity Checks

The app performs local checks on startup to detect whether the device is rooted or running a modified OS. This protects the purchase system integrity. The results of these checks are not transmitted anywhere — they are evaluated entirely on-device.

4. Your Rights

Since data is stored locally, you have full control over:

  • Right of Access: All records are visible within the app.
  • Right to Deletion: You can delete individual records or uninstall the app to wipe all data.
  • Right to Portability: Export sales history to Excel (.xlsx) (Premium feature).

5. Data Retention

Data Location Retained until
Receipts, settings, tasks Local Device (SQLite) In-app deletion or Uninstall
Feedback submissions Google Forms Deleted periodically
Purchase records Google Play Per Google's policy

6. Security

All user data is stored locally and never transmitted to Sholobit servers. Premium tokens are stored in encrypted secure storage (flutter_secure_storage). The local SQLite database security depends on your device's screen lock.

7. Third-Party Analytics

The App does not include any third-party analytics SDK (Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.) and does not include any crash reporting SDK. Analytics are generated entirely from local data.
